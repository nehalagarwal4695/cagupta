Cost Inflation Index Matrix

CBDT notified historical capital acquisition adjustment figures from the base year 2001-02 onward.

The Cost Inflation Index (CII) is used to determine the inflation-adjusted cost of an asset's acquisition. It helps finance teams accurately calculate long-term capital gains tax obligations when selling corporate holdings or real estate assets.
