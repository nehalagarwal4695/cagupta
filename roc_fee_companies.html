ROC Fee Structure (Companies Act)

Structural registration costs, authorized share capital enhancement charges, and late filing fee calculators.

Beyond standard annual form filings, the Companies Act governs core structural transactions, including changes to authorized capital limits and asset charge creation registrations (Form CHG-1). Keeping these records accurate is essential for corporate financial operations.

Core Structural Fees Matrix

Corporate Transaction Category Standard Processing Fee Component Regulatory Timeline Outlines
Corporate Registration (SPICe+ Track) Scaled based on Capital Limits Initial structural setup processing
Asset Charge Registration / Modifications (CHG-1) Standard Tier Fees Applied Must file within 30 days of creation
Registered Office Address Alterations (INC-22) Standard Capital Tier Fee Must file within 30 days of change
Delay Notice: Filing delays beyond 30 days trigger structured late fees, ranging from 2x up to 12x the standard filing cost, along with potential compounding board non-compliance risks.
