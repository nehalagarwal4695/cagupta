Statutory Compliance Calendar

Real-time deadline tracking hub for Tax Year (TY) 2026-27 statutory return filings and regulatory schedules.

07
Every Month

Monthly TDS / TCS Deposit Deadline

Income Tax Act, 1961 Challan ITNS 281

Mandatory online electronic deposit of all tax assets deducted or collected during the preceding month across corporate operations, salaries, and contractor payouts.

Late Consequence
Interest charge tracking from 1% to 1.5% per month levied strictly from the actual date of deduction.
11
Every Month

GSTR-1 Outward Supplies Filing

CGST Legislation, 2017 Form GSTR-1

Statement of outward supplies required for standard monthly taxpayers to upload business-to-business (B2B) invoice entries to enable Input Tax Credit (ITC) flow.

Late Consequence
Statutory late fee tracking of ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST) applied up to capped ceilings.
20
Every Month

GSTR-3B Monthly Consolidated Return

CGST Legislation, 2017 Form GSTR-3B

Consolidated summary return outlining outward supplies, self-assessed input tax credit adjustments, and final cash ledger tax payments due to the government.

Late Consequence
Late fees plus statutory interest calculated at 18% per annum on the net tax liability paid in cash.
15
Jun / Sep / Dec / Mar

Advance Tax Installment Matrix

Income Tax Act, 1961 Challan 280

Quarterly structural tax advancement payouts due for all corporate entities and individuals whose final calculated tax liability exceeds ₹10,000 in a tax year.

Late Consequence
Mandatory addition of interest points under Section 234B and Section 234C modules for shortfalls.
30
Every Year (Oct)

MCA Annual Financial Statement Filing

Companies Act, 2013 Form AOC-4

Filing audited financial statements, balance sheets, profit & loss allocation matrices, and board audit evaluation records with the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

Late Consequence
Strict recurring statutory penalty tracking ledger of ₹100 per day until filing reconciliation.
