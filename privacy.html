Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Gupta Ruchi & Co., we prioritize the security and confidentiality of the financial records and identity parameters entrusted to us. This Privacy Statement details our data collection, processing, and storage protocols.

1. Data Collection Protocols

We collect information directly when you utilize our client/staff desk login forms, submit corporate hiring query sheets, or request consultation parameters. This information may include your corporate entity identity, PAN details, phone records, and email communication addresses.

2. Handling of Calculator Inputs

Any financial figures, tax parameters, salary segments, or loan allocations processed inside our 13-in-1 institutional calculator engines are executed locally within your web browser sessions. Our servers do not capture, log, or store these localized numbers, ensuring complete privacy during your internal auditing checks.

3. Confidentiality and Third-Party Sharing

In accordance with standard professional codes of conduct, your corporate profiles and transaction histories are never shared, traded, or leased to third-party advertising nodes. Data is only shared with authorized statutory government bodies (such as the Income Tax Department, MCA, or GSTN) when explicitly instructed by your management for filing executions.

4. Secure Storage & Cookies

Our platform uses standard temporary session cookies to manage secure authorization states for our client portal portals. This prevents credential hijacking and ensures that access stays strictly limited to verified enterprise users.
