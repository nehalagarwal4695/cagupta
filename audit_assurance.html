Our Multi-Dimensional Practice Framework

We deploy independent verification strategies tailored to distinct market segments, ensuring compliance with evolving legislative adjustments under the Companies Act, Regulations of the ICAI, and SEBI directives.

Statutory Financial Audits

Rigorous examinations of fiscal parameters to ensure financial statements provide a true and fair view. We prepare robust documentation portfolios matching standard corporate presentation formats for presentation to institutional stakeholders, equity blocks, and the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

Internal & Controls Audits

Systematic testing of transaction tracking workflows to map data leaks, prevent security oversights, and test operational bottlenecks. We evaluate the core functionality of your internal financial controls (IFC) to safeguard administrative assets.

Tax Audits (Section 44AB)

Detailed analysis of corporate accounts to guarantee thorough compliance with the parameters of the Income Tax Act, 1961. We handle depreciation allocations, map disallowed expenditure blocks, and file comprehensive Form 3CD declarations seamlessly.

Management & Forensic Audits

Targeted tracking designed to review management performance indicators, map asset misappropriations, trace unauthorized capital movements, and isolate processing non-compliance within internal branches.

Concurrent & Bank Audits

Continuous transaction monitoring setups built to satisfy institutional banking guidelines, covering large cash credit reviews, hypothecated inventory checks, and non-performing asset (NPA) warning mitigations.

Stock & Asset Verifications

Physical inventory valuation audits and fixed-asset structural testing. We align physical inventory counts with digital ledger records to clear up balance sheet discrepancies across production sites.

Methodological Compliance Roadmap

Our operational engagements run on a structured four-phase system aligned with the official Standards on Auditing (SAs) issued by the ICAI. This ensures complete clarity and systematic documentation throughout the verification cycle.

1. Evaluation & Scope Review corporate structures, identify transaction risk zones, map processing controls, and issue the formal engagement brief. SA 210 & SA 300 Parameters
2. Controls Substantiation Execute deep sample checks across transaction vouchers, run automated code ledger tracing, and assess ledger security. SA 315 & SA 330 Compliance
3. Balance Verification Confirm bank balances, verify third-party debtor listings, perform asset calculations, and validate equity structures. SA 501 & SA 505 Frameworks
4. Reporting & Issuance Compile CARO 2020 tracking books, draft independent auditor reviews, configure key audit matters (KAM), and issue official UDIN metrics. SA 700, 705 & 706 Matrix
