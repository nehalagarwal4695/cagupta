Central Sales Tax Rules & Legacy Desk

Inter-state trade validation architectures, pending statutory declaration trackers, and legacy assessment mitigations.

While the implementation of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) subsumed general inter-state trade levies, the Central Sales Tax Act, 1956 remains active for specific commodity sectors—including petroleum products, aviation turbine fuel, and alcoholic liquors for human consumption. Our legacy desk helps companies settle past tax liabilities and manage mandatory tracking parameters.

Core Statutory Reference Points

Section 3: Inter-State Trade Intersections

Defines precisely when a sale or purchase of goods occasions movement across state boundaries or is concluded by transfer of title documentation during transit.

Section 8: Concessional Rate Jurisdictions

Governs corporate eligibility parameters for lower tax rates on inter-state sales when backed by valid statutory certificates issued by purchasing entities.
